# AI Governance Risk Assessment – Resume Screening Systems

This repository contains an independent AI governance risk assessment examining AI-assisted resume screening systems commonly used in Canadian hiring contexts.

The assessment applies the structure and principles of the NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) to explore governance risks related to fairness, transparency, accountability, and stakeholder trust. The document focuses on governance, oversight, and organizational practices rather than technical model evaluation or vendor compliance.

## Contents
- **AI_Governance_Risk_Assessment.pdf** – Full case study document

## Scope and Purpose
This case study was prepared as a professional portfolio artifact to demonstrate applied AI governance and risk analysis skills. It is based on publicly available information and generalized deployment practices and does not evaluate proprietary systems.
